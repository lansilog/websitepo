DUWENDE

Tiny, dwarf-like beings wearing a salakot (traditional Filipino hat). Dwell in their lands and face their wrath.

MANANANGGAL

Eerie flying creature that splits its body in half during night to prey on its victims. Swift, silent, and deadly to those who wander alone.

TIKBALANG

Cunning humanoid creature with horse-like features. Dwells deep in forests, twisting paths to trap travelers in a treacherous maze.

BUNGISNGIS

Colossal one-eyed giant living in dark caves. Its thunderous laughter unleashes destructive sound waves, and its blazing eye fires deadly beams.

DIWATA

A mystical guardian of nature capable of controlling vines, flowers, and forest life. Beautiful yet deadly when angered.

KAPRE

A towering, cigar-smoking giant that rules the forest. Known for its immense strength and cunning, it traps those who dare challenge its domain.
