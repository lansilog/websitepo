PUKSAIN!

Mang George is a lighthearted indie action game by BNB Studio. Play as Mang George and explore colorful stages inspired by Filipino folklore, collect power-ups, and battle quirky enemies.

Fast-paced arena-style boss fight platformer inspired by Cuphead, focusing solely on intense bullet hell battles. The player enters an arena to face a boss, each with multiple phases and evolving attack patterns as its health decreases. To survive, the player can move, jump, and shoot toward the mouse cursor while using a dash mechanic that grants temporary invincibility to dodge incoming attacks. After defeating a boss, the player chooses one of several random upgrades, such as increased damage, reduced dash cooldown, or extra health, before moving on to the next fight. Each encounter escalates in difficulty, with tougher bosses and more complex attack patterns. The cycle continues until the player is defeated, creating a high-stakes, actionpacked experience centered on skillful dodging, precise aiming, and strategic upgrades.
